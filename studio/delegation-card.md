# Delegation Card

## Task
What specific artifact or result are you delegating to the AI?

I'm delegating the research update - keeping up with the latest news and developments of AI by reading the top article on the Tech Crunch AI section (https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/).

## Context
What relevant inputs, files, tools, target audience, or learning preferences should the AI consider?

The AI should consider the input website (https://techcrunch.com/category/artificial-intelligence/), use the tool of the internet to navigate to the first article, see me as the target audience (a computer science master's student), and take my learning preferences into account (I like concise, hierarchical bullets) 

## Success criteria
What is the observable check or metric that defines a successful run?

Observable check is that the title of the first article is clearly displayed, and then a bulleted concise hierarchical summary is displayed underneath it. 

## Restrictions
Are there any prohibited actions, resource limits, or human approval boundaries to enforce?

The only restriction is that there should not be more than 3 "levels" of bullets (one level is a deeper version of another level), and that there cannot be more than 10 bullets total.
